Detector input boost

The Master Level changes listening volume AFTER the detector. This control boosts the waveform for note recognition only; it does not make speakers louder, alter the MP3, or improve separation quality.

Soft mode + 8× accepts levels down to approximately 0.023% RMS; quiet noise can also be detected.

Soft mode can detect lower-amplitude notes, but overlapping instruments and separation bleed still cause errors. The full-song option plays the recording in real time.

Our song reconstruction rules

Keep the original full recordings and lossless FLAC exports as masters. These stem files are separated reference audio, not newly performed instruments. The goal is to recover actual notes, swing, articulations, riffs, fills and timing before independently rebuilding any part.
